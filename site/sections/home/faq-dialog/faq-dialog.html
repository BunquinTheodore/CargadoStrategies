Questions Everyone Asks First

Why Is It Free for Me?

Partners may pay us a fee if you become their client. You never pay us for an introduction.

Do I Have to Hire Them?

No. You decide what happens next. Partners are independent businesses, and each one is responsible for their own licence and advice.

How Do You Choose Who to Show Me?

By distance only. We show the nearest partners for the service you need, closest first. No one pays to be listed higher, and a listing is not a recommendation or an endorsement.

What Happens to My Information?

We use it to answer you and connect you with a suitable partner. We ask for your city so we can find someone close to you. Cargado staff and the partner we connect you with see it, and we do not sell it. Read the privacy policy.
