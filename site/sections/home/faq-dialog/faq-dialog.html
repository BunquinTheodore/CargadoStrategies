Questions Everyone Asks First

Why Is It Free for Me?

Partners may pay Cargado Strategies a fee if you become their client. You never pay Cargado Strategies for an introduction.

Do I Have to Hire Them?

No. You decide what happens next. Partners are independent businesses, and each one is responsible for their own licence and advice.

How Do You Choose Who to Show Me?

By distance only. Cargado Strategies shows the nearest partners for the service you need, closest first. No one pays to be listed higher, and a listing is not a recommendation or an endorsement.

What Happens to My Information?

Cargado Strategies uses it to answer you and connect you with a suitable partner. It asks for your city so it can find someone close to you. Cargado staff and the partner you are connected with see it, and Cargado Strategies does not sell it. Read the privacy policy.
