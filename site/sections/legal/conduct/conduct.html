Partner Code of Conduct

Updated October 20, 2026

Every partner agrees to these commitments when they apply.

Become a Partner →

  1. Describe your licence, credentials and services honestly, and keep your licence current.

  2. Put clients before transactions, and explain any fee or commission before work begins.

  3. Protect client confidentiality and personal information.

  4. Reply to inquiries within 1 business day.

  5. Never promise results you cannot guarantee, and follow your regulator's advertising and referral rules.

  6. Accept removal from the network if these commitments are broken.
