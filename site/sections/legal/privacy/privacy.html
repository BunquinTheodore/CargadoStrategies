Privacy Policy

Updated October 25, 2026

Privacy contact: Lee Holgado, info@cargadostrategies.com

  1. Information Collected

    Your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, city or area, and the message you send. When you book a consultation, also the time you choose, the service it is about and your note. When you create an account, also your profile, bookings, points history and any referral code. When you apply as a partner, the details in your application.

  2. Why It Is Collected

    To answer your inquiry, book your consultation, run your account and points, review partner applications, and connect you with a suitable partner. Cargado Strategies asks for your city so that it can find someone close to you.

  3. Who Sees It

    Cargado staff and the partner you are connected with. Cargado Strategies does not sell your information.

  4. Where It Is Kept

    In Cargado Strategies' lead records and with the services that run this website: Google Firebase (accounts, sign-in, and records of profiles, bookings, points, referrals and partner applications), Google Calendar and Google Meet (consultation bookings and meeting links) and Resend (confirmation emails). These services may store information outside Canada. Facebook and WhatsApp apply their own terms to messages sent through them.

  5. How Long It Is Kept

    Cargado Strategies keeps your information for up to 24 months after its last contact with you, or while your account stays open, unless the law requires it to be kept longer. Then it is deleted securely.

  6. Your Rights

    You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or close your account, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Contact Lee Holgado at info@cargadostrategies.com, and Cargado Strategies will respond within 30 days. It follows the privacy laws that apply in Canada and in your province.

  7. Messages

    Cargado Strategies sends you messages only with your consent, and each marketing message includes a way to unsubscribe. Booking confirmations and account emails are sent only because you asked for them.

  8. Cookies and Analytics

    Cargado Strategies uses no advertising cookies and no analytics tools. Your browser keeps a few small settings on your own device, which Cargado Strategies cannot see. When you sign in, Firebase also keeps your sign-in state on your device so you stay signed in.

  9. Accounts and Sign-In

    You can create an account with an email address and password, or continue with Google, in which case Google shares your name and email address with Cargado Strategies. Passwords are handled by Firebase and are never visible to Cargado Strategies.
