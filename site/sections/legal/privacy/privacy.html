Privacy Policy

Updated October 20, 2026

Privacy contact: Lee Holgado, malee.holgado@mygreatway.ca

  1. Information We Collect

    Your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, city or area, and the message you send us.

  2. Why We Collect It

    To answer your inquiry and connect you with a suitable partner. We ask for your city so that we can find someone close to you.

  3. Who Sees It

    Cargado staff and the partner we connect you with. We do not sell your information.

  4. Where It Is Kept

    In our lead records. Facebook and WhatsApp apply their own terms to messages sent through them.

  5. How Long We Keep It

    We keep your information for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Then we delete it securely.

  6. Your Rights

    You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Contact Lee Holgado at malee.holgado@mygreatway.ca, and we will respond within 30 days. We follow the privacy laws that apply in Canada and in your province.

  7. Messages

    We send you messages only with your consent, and each marketing message includes a way to unsubscribe.

  8. Cookies and Analytics

    We use no advertising cookies and no analytics tools. Your browser keeps a few small settings on your own device, such as your sound and motion choices, and we cannot see them.
