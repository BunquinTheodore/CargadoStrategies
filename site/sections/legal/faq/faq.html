Why Is It Free for Me?

Partners may pay us a fee if you become their client. You never pay us for an introduction.

Do I Have to Hire Them?

No. You decide what happens next. An introduction does not create a professional relationship with Cargado Strategies or with the partner, and you are free to speak with anyone else.

How Are Partners Listed?

By distance only, closest first. A listing is not an endorsement or a recommendation. Where a licence applies, each partner shows it and who issued it, and any sponsored listing is clearly labelled.

Partners are independent businesses. Each one applies, is reviewed and approved by the Cargado team, and is responsible for their own services and licence.

What Happens to My Information?

We use your name, contact details, city or area and your message to answer you and connect you with a partner close to you. We ask for your city only to find someone near you. Cargado staff and the partner we connect you with see it, and we do not sell it.

You may ask to see, correct or delete it at any time. Read the privacy policy.

Does Cargado Give Advice?

Not through this website. Cargado Strategies does not provide legal, mortgage, insurance, real estate, tax, manufacturing or financial advice here. Advice comes from the partner you choose.

The founder's own businesses (insurance, business strategy and outsourcing) are named as hers. Her strategy service is strategy and coaching only — not licensed accounting, tax or investment advice.

How Do I Become a Partner?

Apply on the Become a Partner page with your business details, category, licence number and issuing body, and service area. The Cargado team reviews and approves each application, and every partner agrees to the code of conduct.

Membership buys a listing and network tools only. It does not promise referrals or outcomes.
