Questions Before You Apply

What Do You Ask of Partners?
  • Hold a current licence where one applies.
  • Reply to introductions within 1 business day.
  • Describe their services honestly.
  • Follow the Partner Code of Conduct.

Cargado Strategies reviews and approves each application. A listing goes live only after approval.

What Happens After I Apply?
  1. You send the application. Nothing is sent until you tap send.
  2. Cargado Strategies reviews your licence details and contacts your references.
  3. Cargado Strategies replies within 5 business days.
  4. If approved, your listing goes live.
How Are Partners Listed?

Lists are sorted by distance only. No bidding, no paid ranking, and a listing is never a recommendation.

Cargado Strategies shows them the nearest partners for that service, sorted by distance, with a short WhatsApp introduction. They choose who to talk to.

Do You Promise Clients?

Cargado Strategies doesn't promise a number of introductions, clients or results. That depends on what people near you ask for.
