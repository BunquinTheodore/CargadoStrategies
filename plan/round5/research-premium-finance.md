# Round 5 research — premium, trust-heavy finance and luxury sites

Researched: 2026-10-17. Method: WebFetch of live homepages, plus WebSearch where a site blocked fetching (403: Rolex, Aesop, Lemonade, Questrade; McKinsey timed out). Fetches return extracted text, not the rendered page, so colours and fonts are only stated where a source gives them. Items marked **[known]** come from prior knowledge of the site rather than this session's fetch; treat them as reasonable but unverified.

Audience reminder: the client's buyers are 30 to 40 years old, Canadian, and want insurance, business strategy or outsourcing from a person they can trust. The ask is "modern and wow, but quick and not clogged".

---

## 1. Site-by-site findings

### 1.1 Wealthsimple (wealthsimple.com/en-ca) — Canadian, modern finance
- **Layout:** about 10 stacked blocks, **one product per screen**: eyebrow label, one-line H2, one image or video. Several blocks have **no CTA at all**; the image and headline do the work.
- **Hero:** a single statement headline ("Something Good Is Going to Happen") with one CTA.
- **Services:** a five-item product index near the top (name plus a tagline), then one full screen per product.
- **Trust:** one big number at the end ("Trusted by over 4 million Canadians"), with a dated, sourced growth claim. No testimonials and no press wall.
- **Typography [known]:** a serif display face for headlines over a quiet sans for body. This is the closest proof that a serif display reads as modern to 30 to 40 year old Canadians.
- **Lesson for Cargado:** "One screen = one product + one sentence + one visual" is exactly the Round 4 decision. Put the proof number at the close, not scattered.

### 1.2 Ethos (ethos.com) — insurtech, life insurance
- **Hero:** benefit headline ("Life insurance for any budget"), a one-line sub (no exam, online), one primary CTA ("Check my price"), and a row of small award badges.
- **Price:** never a real price, only "from $30/month" with the quote framed as personal. **This is the model for our [price] placeholder:** "From [$X] / month, confirmed after a short call."
- **Structure:** three value cards, then three-step "how it works", a comparison table, an FAQ that starts with three entry questions before the full list, and a closing CTA.
- **Trust:** a Trustpilot score with review count, "half a million families", carrier logos, and a footnoted, paid-spokesperson disclosure.
- **Footer:** licence numbers, an "issued by carriers" note, and a "not a law firm, no legal/tax advice" disclaimer. This is directly parallel to our "strategy and coaching only" line.

### 1.3 Policygenius (policygenius.com) — broker / marketplace (closest business model)
- **Hero:** "A *human* approach to buying insurance". One italic word does the emotional work. A family photo sits beside a product picker of four tiles ("What can we help you find?").
- **Independence copy:** "an insurance *marketplace*, not an insurance *company*"; not owned by any insurer; "insurers pay a commission already included in the premium, no added fee"; "we will disclose the commission on a specific policy if asked". **This copy sits in the FAQ, not hidden in the footer.** It is the template for Cargado's commission and independence notice.
- **Trust:** two rating sources with counts, four big stats, five short named testimonials, and "363 days a year of human support".
- **Referral programme:** a banner offering a gift card per referred friend with a cap and "terms apply". This is the pattern for the partner programme teaser.
- **Close:** "Finished scrolling? Start saving." plus one button. The tone is witty but plain.

### 1.4 Lemonade (lemonade.com) — insurtech (via secondary sources; site 403)
- **Flow:** "Check our prices", then a conversational bot (Maya, about 13 questions, about 90 seconds), then the quote, and only then the email. **Value comes before asking for contact details.**
- **Microcopy:** context-aware questions; a notice appears only once it is relevant. The quote feels "not like an insurance form".
- **Lesson:** our WhatsApp-first CTA is the human version of this. A pre-filled message ("Hi, I'd like to talk about [service]") gives the same "one tap, no form" feel. The application form should feel conversational: one question group per step, with plain labels.

### 1.5 Pictet (pictet.com) — Swiss private bank, heritage
- **Hero:** one word plus a definition ("Nurturing. The practice of protecting money as it grows."), one "Find out more" link, and an optional video.
- **Trust:** heritage ("Since 1805"), a history timeline, and four dated headline figures.
- **Lesson:** a **definition-style headline** suits an advisory brand ("Strategy. The practice of …"). It is calm, confident and short. The founder story can use a timeline as its spine.

### 1.6 Coutts (coutts.com) — UK private bank
- **Hero:** "Personal. Expert. For all your wealth needs." Three short words, a heritage sub (1692), and one CTA ("Join Coutts"), repeated through the page.
- **Services:** three cards (heading, one line, "Explore"). Then "The Coutts difference" in three cards, history, and awards grouped by category.
- **Two-audience split:** "Already a client?" is a separate path. This maps to our split between clients and partners.
- **Footer:** registered office, regulator, firm reference number, and an "Important information" link.

### 1.7 Rothschild & Co (rothschildandco.com) — independent advisory group
- **Hero:** an ambient looping video still and a positioning headline: "One of the world's leading **independent** financial services groups." They lead with independence, as Cargado should.
- **Services:** "Three complementary businesses" in three image cards with one text link each. This matches Cargado exactly (insurance, strategy, outsourcing).
- **Trust:** bold numerals and dated footnotes. A **fraud-awareness notice** sits on the homepage, which is worth copying in a light form for a WhatsApp-based business ("We will only ever contact you from [number]").
- **Close:** "Contact us to begin the conversation."

### 1.8 Bessemer Trust (bessemertrust.com) — US family office
- **Extreme restraint:** a full-width portrait of a real employee, the headline "Enhancing private wealth / for generations" split over two lines, and **no hero button**. Services live only in the menu.
- **Contact:** link-based "Contact Us", no form on the homepage.
- **Lesson:** a real human photograph plus a two-line serif headline is the most trusted hero in the category. For Cargado: **put the founder's portrait in the hero**, or one screen later, instead of only abstract 3D.

### 1.9 Hamilton Lane (hamiltonlane.com) — private markets
- **Hero:** name, category line, a **three-stat bar** and a two-part tagline ("Client-driven. Results-oriented.").
- **Services:** five equal cards (heading, one sentence, "Learn More").
- **Caution:** the headline number hides a footnote nuance. Our numbers must be honest, so we use [placeholders] until the founder confirms them.

### 1.10 Rolex (rolex.com) **[known]**; fetch returned 403
- Full-bleed product film and photography, very few words per screen, and a thin sans for UI over a classical serif wordmark. Dark green and gold, huge whitespace, slow cross-fades rather than flashy motion. The navigation stays minimal and always reachable.
- **Lesson:** "wow" comes from **scale and pacing**: one huge image or object and one line of type. It does not come from many moving parts. Motion is slow (600 to 1200 ms), eased, and never bouncy.

### 1.11 Cartier (cartier.com)
- **Hero:** a two-word evocative title ("Endless love") and one "Discover" CTA.
- **Layout:** alternating image and text feature modules (2048×864 banners, 1080×1080 squares), then three square **service tiles** (services, appointments).
- **Lesson:** the "Book an appointment" style of tile is the luxury equivalent of our WhatsApp CTA. Phrase ours as an appointment ("Book a 15-minute call on WhatsApp"), not as "chat".

### 1.12 Aesop (aesop.com) — via design-system write-ups; site 403
- Warm ivory ground (#fffef2), charcoal text, a single neo-grotesque (Suisse Int'l) for nearly everything, **no shadows, gradients or rounded corners**, and no second accent colour. Product copy reads "like a label, not an advertisement".
- **Lesson:** our bone (ivory) field should look like this: flat, quiet and typographic. Gold appears only as a hairline rule or a small accent, never as a gradient on the bone field.

### 1.13 Bang & Olufsen (bang-olufsen.com)
- **Hero:** one product, a poetic line ("For every place you want to be.") and one sub sentence.
- **Products:** a quiet list with name and price; lifestyle images in material tones.
- **Lesson:** list offers as **name, one line, "from [price]"**, with no cards stuffed with bullet points.

### 1.14 Stripe (stripe.com) — reference for "wow without clutter"
- **Hero:** a short headline, one sentence and a logo strip directly underneath, so **the claim and the proof sit together**. One recurring animated brand shape (the gradient wave) with a static fallback.
- **CTA pair:** a primary action plus a lower-friction alternative, with the pattern repeated at the bottom.
- **Lesson:** our wow element is the **one gold network globe**, used consistently, with a static fallback. CTA pair: "Message on WhatsApp" (primary) and "See how it works" (secondary).

### 1.15 Usability research (NN/g)
- **Older-user guidelines:** People in their 40s already need larger type than younger users. Avoid light, low-contrast text. Use large click targets. Accept any input format (phone with or without dashes). Write plain, adjacent error messages. Never play surprise sound. Website ability declines about 0.8% a year from age 25 to 60.
- **Scrolljacking:** it disorients users, and long scroll-jacked sections look broken. Text inside scroll-jacked sections is the worst case. If it is used: keep it short, keep vertical scrolling, keep navigation sticky, keep the key CTA above the fold, and avoid it by default on mobile. **This is the biggest risk in the current film engine.** Round 4's "one scroll = one section" (snap, not scrub-reading) is the right fix. Text must be fully readable when a section settles, never mid-animation.

## 2. Cross-cutting patterns (what the best do)

| Pattern | Seen at | Apply to Cargado |
|---|---|---|
| One idea per screen: eyebrow, one headline, one sentence, one visual, at most one CTA | Wealthsimple, Rolex, B&O, Bessemer | Round 4 one-screen sections; cap each at **25 to 40 words of body** |
| Headline of 2 to 6 words, often a triad or a definition | Coutts, Pictet, Hamilton Lane | "Insure. Advise. Delegate." or "Strategy. The practice of…" |
| The italic word as the emotional accent | Policygenius ("*human*") | Times New Roman italic on one word in gold; Title Case elsewhere |
| A real human photograph in or near the hero | Bessemer, Policygenius, Ethos | Founder portrait (duotone, black and gold) on screen 2 |
| Three-up services | Rothschild, Coutts | Three services, as three tall panels or a three-row index |
| "From [price]" plus "confirmed after a short call" | Ethos, B&O | Offer pages: "From [$X]", then what is included, then the CTA |
| Independence and commission stated plainly in the FAQ, not only the footer | Policygenius, Rothschild | Keep the compliance notice; also add an FAQ answer "How do we get paid?" |
| Proof as a few big numerals with dates and sources | Wealthsimple, Pictet, Hamilton Lane | 3 numerals, [placeholder] until confirmed, footnoted |
| Testimonials: short, named, with a role | Policygenius, Stripe | 3 quotes, one per screen or one rotating, never a wall |
| Fraud and contact-safety notice | Rothschild | "We only message from [WhatsApp number]" in the contact section and footer |
| Repeated single CTA in the same wording | Coutts, Ethos | "Message Us on WhatsApp" everywhere, the same label and the same gold button |
| No shadows or gradients on light fields; hairline rules | Aesop | The bone field is flat; gold hairlines at 1px |
| Motion is slow, eased, one hero object | Rolex, Stripe | Globe plus iris only; drop the extra decorative motion |
| Licence and regulator text in the footer, small but present | Coutts, Ethos, Policygenius | Footer compliance block (see recipe) |

**Typography pairings seen with a serif display:**
- Serif display with a neo-grotesque or humanist sans (Wealthsimple, Coutts, Rolex). Ours: **Times New Roman (display, Title Case), Manrope (body), Poppins (UI/labels)**. This is valid. Keep Poppins only for small caps labels and buttons, so there are not three competing voices.
- Size discipline for a 30 to 45 audience: body **18px minimum on desktop, 17px on mobile**, line-height 1.6, measure 60 to 70ch. Display H1 clamp(44px, 7vw, 104px) with tight leading (1.02 to 1.08) and tracking of -0.01em. Eyebrow 13 to 14px Poppins, 0.14em tracking, uppercase or small caps (eyebrows are labels, so Title Case does not apply to them, but check this against the brand rule). Never set body copy in grey below 4.5:1. Ivory #F3ECDD on #0B0B0C is fine; gold #D4AF37 on black is fine for large text and labels; on bone use gold-dark #7A5C12.

## 3. Layout recipes for Cargado (black and gold, one screen per section)

Global rules for every recipe: a 12-column grid, max content width 1200px, side gutters clamp(20px, 5vw, 64px). Vertical rhythm is set by the viewport (each section is 100svh with content centred or anchored to the bottom third). **One** primary CTA per screen at most, and a minimum touch target of 48px. All text is static and fully legible once a section settles; animate only the entrance (300 to 700 ms, ease-out), never while the user is reading.

### 3.1 Hero (home, screen 1) — "the wow"
- **Field:** ink. The gold network globe is large, bleeding off the right edge (it fills about 55% of the width on desktop), slowly rotating (one revolution about every 60 s).
- **Left column (cols 1 to 6), bottom-anchored:**
  - Eyebrow (Poppins 13px gold): "Canadian Referral Network · Est. [Year]"
  - H1 (Times New Roman, Title Case, about 96px): "Trusted Advice, *Connected* Across Canada" (one italic gold word)
  - Sub (Manrope 19px, ivory at 80% opacity, maximum 2 lines): "Insurance, business strategy and outsourcing, from one person you can actually reach."
  - CTA pair: [Message Us on WhatsApp] (solid gold, black text) and "See How It Works ↓" (a text link with a gold underline).
- **Proof strip** directly under the CTA, small: "[N] partners · [N] provinces · Replies within [X] hours". The claim and the proof sit together, as at Stripe.
- **Wow moment:** on load, the globe's points draw in, then a single gold arc connects two cities. That happens once and takes about 1.5 s; the user can skip it by scrolling. No HUD, no counters.
- **Mobile:** the globe sits above the text (top 45% of the screen), the type drops to 48px, and the CTA is full width.

### 3.2 Services overview (home screen 3, and services.html intro)
- **Pattern:** a "Three complementary services" index (Rothschild and Coutts).
- **Desktop:** three tall vertical panels side by side, filling the screen, each separated by a 1px gold hairline. Each panel holds a big numeral (Times New Roman, 120px, gold outline or 20% opacity) "01 / 02 / 03", the service name as H3 (Title Case), one sentence, and "Explore →".
- **Hover/focus:** the panel widens (flex 1 to 1.4) and reveals a monochrome image. A single, transform-only animation.
- **Mobile:** three stacked rows, each with numeral, name, sentence and arrow. No cards and no shadows.
- **Rule:** no bullets on this screen. Bullets live on the offer detail screen.

### 3.3 Offer detail with pricing placeholder (services.html, one screen per offer)
- **Field:** alternate bone and ink between offers so each offer reads as its own "room".
- **Two-column split:**
  - **Left (cols 1 to 5):** eyebrow "Insurance · Offer 01", H2 offer name, a 2-sentence promise.
  - **Right (cols 7 to 12):** a "label card" in the Aesop style. A flat card with a 1px gold border, no shadow, holding:
    - "From [$X] / month" (Times New Roman 48px) and a small "Confirmed after a 15-minute call" (Ethos pattern).
    - "Included" with at most 4 lines and gold check glyphs.
    - "Best for:" with one line.
    - CTA: [Ask About This on WhatsApp], with the message pre-filled to name the offer.
- **Compliance line** directly under the card in 14px muted text: commission notice / "strategy and coaching only", depending on the offer.
- An optional "How it works" sub-screen: three numbered steps in a horizontal row (Ethos and Policygenius): Message, Short call, Plan in [X] days.

### 3.4 Founder story (story.html)
- **Pattern:** Bessemer portrait plus Pictet timeline.
- **Screen 1:** a full-bleed duotone founder portrait (black and gold), with the headline bottom-left in two lines: "Built on One Promise: / *Answer Every Message*" (example, Title Case). There is no button.
- **Screens 2 to 4:** a timeline with one milestone per screen. A large year in gold Times New Roman sits left, and 2 to 3 sentences sit right. A thin vertical gold line runs through the screens and draws as you advance.
- **Screen 5:** a pull quote from the founder in large italic serif, with name, role and signature (an SVG signature if one exists).
- **Screen 6 (close):** "Let's Talk" with the WhatsApp CTA and the founder's small photo as an avatar beside the button. This is a personal, human CTA.

### 3.5 Partner programme (partners.html)
- **Pattern:** the Policygenius referral banner, expanded into a short sequence, plus Coutts' "Already a client?" split.
- **Screen 1:** H1 "Become a Cargado Partner", with a sub that names who it is for (advisors, accountants, consultants in Canada).
- **Screen 2: "How the partnership works"** in three steps: Refer, We Serve, You Earn [commission placeholder]. Put the commission and independence notice **right here**, at 15px, not only in the footer.
- **Screen 3: "What you get"** in a 2×2 grid of short benefits (one icon or numeral each, one line each).
- **Screen 4:** the application form (3.6).
- **Screen 5:** an FAQ accordion of 5 to 6 questions, the first being "How do partners get paid?". It uses native `<details>`, so it is accessible and needs no JS.

### 3.6 Application form
- **Pattern:** Lemonade's conversational flow, with NN/g's forgiving inputs.
- **Layout:** a single column at most 560px wide, centred on the bone field, with labels above the fields (Manrope 16px semi-bold) and inputs 56px tall with 18px text.
- **Steps:** 3 short steps with a step indicator ("Step 1 of 3 · About You"): (1) name, email, phone (any format accepted); (2) business or practice, province, how you would refer; (3) a free-text note and the consent checkbox with the compliance text.
- **Errors:** inline, under the field, in plain words ("Please add a phone number so we can reach you"). Colour plus an icon, never colour alone.
- **Submit:** "Send Application". The confirmation screen reads "Thank you, [Name]. We'll message you on WhatsApp within [X] hours", with a secondary "Prefer to chat now? Message us" CTA.
- **Fallback:** an always-visible "Rather talk? Message us on WhatsApp" link above the form.

### 3.7 Legal pages (legal.html)
- **No film here:** normal scrolling, no Lenis, no stage. NN/g: don't scroll-jack text-heavy content.
- **Layout:** a sticky left table of contents (desktop, cols 1 to 3) and the content at a 68ch measure (cols 5 to 11). On the bone field, set the body at 18px with a line-height of 1.7.
- **Structure:** H1 "Legal & Disclosures", a "Last updated [date]" line, and anchored H2 sections: Independence & Commission, Strategy and Coaching Only, Privacy, Terms, Contact & Fraud Notice. Each [bracket] placeholder stays visible, highlighted with a subtle gold-dark underline.
- Add a "Back to top" link and print styles.

### 3.8 Footer (all pages)
- **Field:** ink, with a 1px gold top rule.
- **Row 1:** a big closing line (Times New Roman 56px), "Ready When You Are.", with the WhatsApp CTA on the right (the Coutts and Rothschild "begin the conversation" pattern).
- **Row 2:** four columns: Services (3 links), Company (Story, Partners), Contact (WhatsApp number, email, [City], Canada; "We only message from this number" as the fraud line), and Follow (Facebook).
- **Row 3, compliance block (14px, ivory at 65% opacity, which must still pass 4.5:1):**
  - the commission and independence notice
  - "Strategy and coaching only; not legal, tax or accounting advice"
  - [licence / registration placeholder]
  - © [Year] Cargado Strategies · Privacy · Terms · Accessibility
- **Constraints:** no floating WhatsApp button and no phone bar (Round 4); the footer CTA plus each section's CTA cover the need.

## 4. Top takeaways for the build team
1. **Wow equals scale, pacing and one hero object**, not more effects. Keep the globe and the iris; cut everything decorative (already decided in Round 4).
2. **One idea per screen** with a hard word budget: a headline of 6 words or fewer, a sub of 25 words or fewer, and one CTA.
3. **A human face beats abstraction** for trust: add the founder's portrait early.
4. **State independence and commission in plain words in context** (the Policygenius model), as well as in the footer.
5. **Price as "From [$X]", confirmed after a short call** (the Ethos model).
6. **Read-safe motion:** text is never animated while it is being read, sections snap, navigation stays visible, and legal pages are not scroll-jacked.
7. **Type for 30 to 45 year olds:** 18px body, high contrast, 48px targets, and forgiving forms.
